Our story

Courage, worn every day.

शौर्य means courage, valour and strength. It was never about armour. It was about the quiet confidence of a person who knows who they are.

SHAURYA brings that spirit to the accessories a man wears every day. We draw on India's long tradition of leather and metal craft — the arches of palace windows, the precision of the armourer's workshop — and build it into belts and wallets made for a modern life.

I

Indian roots

Forms and details drawn from Indian architecture and metalwork, interpreted without cliché.

II

Modern craftsmanship

Full-grain leathers, hand-finished edges and buckles designed in-house.

III

Individual identity

Personalised buckles and monograms, so each piece carries its owner's name.
