FAQ

How accurate is the 3D engraving preview?

It's a close approximation. Our team reviews every design and contacts you if anything needs adjusting before production.

What artwork can I upload?

PNG, JPG, WebP and SVG preview in the studio; AI, EPS and PDF can be attached for review. Simple, high-contrast artwork engraves best. Photographs often cannot be reproduced on metal.

Can I buy the magnetic belt?

Not yet. It is a concept under development. You can register for updates on the magnetic belt page.

Do you do corporate orders?

Yes — request a quotation from the personalisation studio or contact us above.
