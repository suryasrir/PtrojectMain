Designed to reimagine the way you wear a belt.

The SHAURYA magnetic belt is a design concept in research and development. It is not patented, tested, certified or commercially available. Visuals and animations illustrate the intended design only and do not represent verified magnetic strength, safety or durability.
Concept overview

No buckle. A single diagonal line.

The idea: a black leather belt whose two ends are cut on a diagonal and meet edge to edge at the front. Magnetic components concealed between leather layers guide the ends together, leaving a clean seam marked by a fine metal inlay.

Design exploration

Proposed construction

  • Outer layer: full-grain leather, diagonal cut
  • Magnet carrier: a thin housing holding three disc magnets per end
  • Keeper plate: thin steel plate to spread and guide the magnetic pull
  • Lining: soft leather lining against the wearer
  • Seam: fine metal inlay marking the closure line
Questions we're working on
  • Holding force under everyday load and movement
  • Ease of release, including for users with limited grip
  • Size adjustment without holes
  • Safety near cards, phones and medical devices
  • Durability of leather and housings over time
