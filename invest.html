01 — Vision & mission

A men's accessories house with Indian roots and international standards.

Vision: to become the Indian men's accessories brand that the next generation is proud to wear and to gift.

Mission: design distinctive belts, wallets and personalised accessories with honest materials, and make them easy to explore, customise and buy online.

02 — Brand story & identity

शौर्य — courage, valour, strength.

A premium black, antique gold and ivory identity; a shield-and-arch S emblem inspired by royal insignia and metal craft; a serif-led editorial voice. Designed to sit comfortably beside international brands while staying recognisably Indian.

05 — Personalisation technology

Personalisation is the differentiator.

Customers design online: model, leather, finish, text, typeface or logo — previewed live in 3D. Designs are saved and submitted with artwork for technical review, then engraved to order.

  • Live 3D engraving preview in the browser
  • Logo upload with review workflow
  • Saved designs and quotation requests
  • Supports individual and corporate orders
Try the studio
06 — Manufacturing strategy

Make in India, step by step.

  • Phase 1: partner with established leather workshops; in-house design and quality control
  • Phase 2: in-house engraving and finishing equipment for personalised orders
  • Phase 3: expand in-house cutting and assembly as volumes justify
  • Buckle tooling developed for SHAURYA's own designs
